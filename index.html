College basketball scouting reports

R, Shiny, Plotly

Real data. About 540,000 shots from 2,016 players in the 2025-26 season, from ESPN play-by-play. The shots I parse match 98.8% of box-score attempts.

  • Builds a scouting report for any player with 150 or more shots: efficiency shot map, zone mix against the league, and percentiles.
  • Separates shot quality (expected points from where a player shoots) from shot-making (actual minus expected, shrunk for small samples).
  • Groups players into six archetypes with k-means and matches each player to the closest college and 2025-26 NBA profiles by role.
